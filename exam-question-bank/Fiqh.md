## Q001

**题目 (Question):**

The essential parts of the ijarah (hiring, rental) transaction include:

**答案 (Answer):**

both of these.

> 💡 助记：ijarah（租赁/雇佣）合同的两大要素 = ① 双方当事人（the two parties）+ ② 报酬/费用（payment or fee）。

---

## Q002

**题目 (Question):**

One of the essential parts of an ijarah (hiring, rental) contract is the wording or format (offer and acceptance). (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

True.

> 助记：ijarah 的要素之一 = 要约与承诺（offer and acceptance）的表述/形式。

---

## Q003

**题目 (Question):**

One of the conditions of the ijarah contract is mutual consent between the two parties to the contract. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

True.

> 助记：ijarah 的条件之一 = 双方「相互同意」（mutual consent）。

## Q004

The ijarah (hiring, rental) transaction may have to do with:

**答案 (Answer):**

both of these.

## Q010

**题目 (Question):**

The eenah transaction:

**答案 (Answer):**

is neither of these.

> 说明：eenah 交易（即“艾奈”交易，用形式合法的买卖掩盖 riba）——既**非**「经训从未提及」，也**非**「古兰直接明文禁止」，而是通过圣训/学者判定的欺诈性交易，故答案为 neither of these。

---

## Q011

Prohibited transactions include:

**答案 (Answer):**

the eenah transaction.

> 助记：被禁止的交易包括 **eenah 交易**（分期付款销售则本身合法，不算被禁）。

Ijarah (renting or hiring):

## Q016

**题目 (Question):**

The one who rents a house to use as a dwelling, has the right to use it for any other purpose even if the landlord has not given his consent. (True/False)

False.

> 说明：租屋作住宅者，**无权**未经房东同意改作其他用途。原句为假。

## Q018
