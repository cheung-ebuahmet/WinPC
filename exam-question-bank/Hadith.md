## Q001

**题目 (Question):**

One of the attributes that cannot be ascribed to Allah Almighty is forgetfulness. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

True.

> 助记：安拉绝不会「遗忘」（forgetfulness），这是不能归于安拉的属性。

---

## Q002

**题目 (Question):**

The basic principle regarding acts of worship is that they are not allowed unless proof has been established for them. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

True.

> 助记：敬拜类（ibadat）的基本法则 = 除非有证据（proof/dalil）确立，否则一律不允许。对比习俗类（adat）则是「除非禁止否则允许」。

---

## Q003

**题目 (Question):**

In the Prophet's ﷺ statement "He has prohibited some things, so do not come near them. He has set limits, so do not transgress them," what is meant by limits is obligatory duties only. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

False.

> 说明：此圣训中的「limits（hudud，界限）」不只指必定义务（obligatory duties），而是涵盖安拉所设定的一切界限（含禁止、规定等），故「仅指必定义务」为假。

---

## Q004

**题目 (Question):**

One way of showing kindness to animals when slaughtering them is using stunning (electric shock). (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

False.

> 说明：善待动物、使屠宰仁慈的方式是（用锋利的刀）迅速放血等符合教法的做法，而非电击致晕（stunning/electric shock）——电击不被视为善待动物的正确方式，故为假。

---

## Q005

**题目 (Question):**

According to Islamic teachings, ihsan (kindness) means showing kindness to all of creation and to all things, even animals. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

True.

> 助记：ihsan（至善/仁慈）涵盖对一切造物、一切事物——**甚至对动物**——的善待。

## Q006

**题目 (Question):**

Protecting the Quran from the distortions of the innovators and the exaggerations of the extremists:

**答案 (Answer):**

is part of sincerity towards the Book of Allah Almighty.

> 助记：保护古兰免遭异端者的歪曲与极端者的夸大，属于「**对安拉经典（Book of Allah）的忠诚/naseehah**」。

## Q007

**题目 (Question):**

If a person eats whilst fasting, because he forgot that he was fasting:

**答案 (Answer):**

he will be excused.

> 助记：斋戒中**因忘记而进食**者，可获宽恕（excused），斋戒不坏。

## Q012

The verse {Allah intends for you ease and does not intend for you hardship} [al-Baqarah 2:185] indicates:

**答案 (Answer):**

both of these.

> 助记：此节经文表明 ① 伊斯兰的目标=求易避难 ② 易（ease）是安拉宗教的基本原则之一——两者皆对。

## Q013

**题目 (Question):**

Whoever introduces into the religion of Allah that which is not part of it – whether it has to do with beliefs, words or actions –:

**答案 (Answer):**

has transgressed the sacred limits of Allah.

> 助记：在安拉宗教中引入不属于它的东西（无论信仰、言语、行为），即**逾越了安拉的神圣界限**。

---

## Q014

**题目 (Question):**

Going to extremes means going beyond the bounds of moderation. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

True.

> 助记：走向极端（extremism/ghuluww）= 越出**适度（moderation）**的界限。

---

## Q015

**题目 (Question):**

Naseehah is a concise word, which is indicative of goodwill and kind actions towards another person. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

True.

> 助记：naseehah（忠诚/忠告）是一个**言简意赅**的词，指向对他人的善意与善行。

---

## Q016

**题目 (Question):**

It is very dangerous to neglect the duty to enjoin what is right and forbid what is wrong, because in that case people will become accustomed to what is wrong. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

True.

> 助记：忽视「劝善戒恶」（enjoin right / forbid wrong）极其危险——人们会**习以为常地作恶**。

---

## Q017

**题目 (Question):**

The Prophet's words in the hadith include: "You must listen and obey at times of hardship and of ease, _______________, and when others are given precedence over you":

**答案 (Answer):**

both of these.

> 助记：圣训「难易之时、…、以及他人被优先于你之时，都要听从服从」——空处填「无论你乐意（inclined）还是勉强（reluctant）」两者皆包含。

---

## Q018

**题目 (Question):**

Showing kindness when killing animals means:

**答案 (Answer):**

making the blade sharp and keeping it out of the view of the animal.

> 助记：屠宰时善待动物 = **磨快刀刃 + 不让动物看到刀**（对比：电击、勒死都不是善待）。

---

## Q019

**题目 (Question):**

In the hadith narrated from an-Nawwas ibn Sam'an (RA), the Prophet's ﷺ statement, "al-birr" (translated as righteousness) is a comprehensive word that includes all types of acts of worship and obedience. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

True.

> 助记：al-birr（至善/虔诚）是**总括性**的词，涵盖一切敬拜与顺从。

---

## Q020

**题目 (Question):**

There is a difference between being ignorant of the ruling and being ignorant of the punishment. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

True.

> 助记：对**判令（ruling）**的无知 ≠ 对**惩罚（punishment）**的无知——两者有区别。
