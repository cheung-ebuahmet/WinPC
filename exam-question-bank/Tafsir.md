Most of the commentators are of the view that Surat al-Alaq was the first surah of the Quran to be revealed. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

## Q002

**题目 (Question):**

The verse {No! Do not obey him} means:

**答案 (Answer):**

neither of these.

> 💡 提示：这节经文出自 Surat al-Alaq（96:19，即 Q001 提到的首降古兰）。"No! Do not obey him" 指不要服从 Abu Jahl——他禁止先知礼拜。两个干扰项（戒斋、断绝亲属关系）都不对，故答案为 neither of these。

## Q003

**题目 (Question):**

The verse {Then let him call his associates} refers to:

**答案 (Answer):**

his people and his tribe.

> 说明：正确指「他的族人与部落」（即他的 nadi——被召唤来帮忙的族人/同伴），**不是**「东西方之人」。原题的两个表述（「东西方之人」=False、「族人与部落」=True）是同一道题，此处合并为一条。

## Q004

**题目 (Question):**

The verse {and to establish prayer}:

**答案 (Answer):**

both of these.

> 助记：「立站拜功（iqamat as-salah）」可理解为 ① 按时礼拜 ② 以最佳方式完成——两者皆对。

## Q005

**题目 (Question):**

The verse {The angels and the Spirit descend therein} means:

**答案 (Answer):**

the angels (peace be upon them) descend a great deal during this night, because it is so blessed, and the 'Spirit' is Jibreel (peace be upon him).

> 助记：此夜（贵夜）天使大量降临，因其至为吉祥；「the Spirit（鲁哈）」= 吉卜利勒（Jibreel）。

## Q006

**题目 (Question):**

The words {For indeed, with hardship [will be] ease, Indeed, with hardship [will be] ease}:

**答案 (Answer):**

both of these.

> 助记：这组经文的重复，既为**增添希望（increase hope）**，也为**确认应许（confirm the promise）**。

## Q007

**题目 (Question):**

The verse {Allah being pleased with them} indicates:

**答案 (Answer):**

the pleasure of Allah is of higher status than enjoying the delights in Paradise that they will be granted.

> 助记：安拉的喜悦（riḍwān）**高于**他们将享有的乐园诸般享受。

---

## Q008

**题目 (Question):**

{No!} is a word of deterrence only. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

False.

> 说明：`{No!}`（kallā）不只是「制止/威慑（deterrence）」之词，还含强调、否定等作用，故「仅是威慑之词」为假。

---

## Q009

**题目 (Question):**

The verse {gardens of perpetual residence beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will abide forever}:

**答案 (Answer):**

both of these.

> 助记：① 意为「永居其中、永不离开的乐园」② 词 adn（'adn）= 停留/居所——两者皆对。

---

## Q010

**题目 (Question):**

The verse {So when you have finished [your duties]} means:

**答案 (Answer):**

when you have finished your daily work and tasks.

> 说明：此节「当你完成（事务）时」指完成**日常的工作与事务**（daily work and tasks），而非完成礼拜。常见误选项是「完成礼拜」。

---

## Q011

**题目 (Question):**

{And raised high for you your renown} – there is no mention in this verse of blessing from Allah to His Prophet ﷺ. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

False.

> 说明：此节「并提升了你的声望」**正是**安拉对其先知 ﷺ 的恩典/祝福（blessing）——故原句「没有提及祝福」为假。

---

## Q012

**题目 (Question):**

Ibn Mas'ud (RA) said: The clearest and most precise verse in the Quran is:

**答案 (Answer):**

{So whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it. And whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it} [az-Zalzalah 99:7-8].

> 助记：Ibn Mas'ud 称最明确精确的经文 = 《地震章》99:7-8「谁做一微粒之善必见之，谁做一微粒之恶必见之」。

---

## Q013

**题目 (Question):**

In the verse {A Messenger from Allah, reciting purified scriptures}, what is meant by the Messenger is Isa (AS). (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

False.

> 说明：此节「安拉的使者，诵读纯净的经典」所指**不是**尔撒（Isa AS），而是指先知穆罕默德 ﷺ（或据注释指其他），故为假。

---

## Q014

**题目 (Question):**

In the verse {Indeed, We sent the Quran down during the Night of Decree}, it was said the word qadr (translated here as decree) refers to might and honour. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

True.

> 助记：「高贵夜（Laylat al-Qadr）」中的 qadr 一解 = **尊贵/荣耀（might and honour）**。

---

## Q015

**题目 (Question):**

In Surat al-Alaq, Allah issued a challenge to Abu Jahl. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

True.

> 助记：Surat al-Alaq 中安拉向 **Abu Jahl** 发出挑战。

## Q016

**题目 (Question):**

The verse {Except for those who believe and do righteous deeds, for they will have a reward uninterrupted}:

**答案 (Answer):**

both of these.

> 助记：此节 ① 是安拉对信而行善者的例外恩典 ② 意为「永不中断的报偿」——两者皆对。

## Q017

**题目 (Question):**

The word dharrah is used to refer to the smallest type of ant. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

True.

> 助记：dharrah（微粒/尘埃）一词，本义也指**最小的一种蚂蚁**。
