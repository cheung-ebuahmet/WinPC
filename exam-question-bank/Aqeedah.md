## Q001

**题目 (Question):**

The ruling on travelling by car is:

**答案 (Answer):**

that it is not an innovation (bidah).

---

## Q002

**题目 (Question):**

The evidence quoted by those who say that there is such a thing as good innovation (bidah hasanah) includes:

**答案 (Answer):**

the words of Umar (RA) regarding Taraweeh: "What a good innovation (bidah) this is."

> ⚠️ 易混点：选项中的圣训 "Whoever introduces into this matter of ours anything that is not part of it, it will be rejected" 是**反对方**（主张不存在 bidah hasanah）的经典依据，别误当作支持 bidah hasanah 的证据。

---

## Q003

**题目 (Question):**

Shaykh al-Islam said: The basic principle with regard to customs is:

**答案 (Answer):**

that none of them are prohibited except that which Allah has forbidden.

> 💡 提示（完整原则，助记）：Shaykh al-Islam 说——"The basic principle with regard to acts of worship is that none of them are permitted except that which Allah has prescribed; and the basic principle with regard to customs is that none of them are prohibited except that which Allah has forbidden."（敬拜类：除安拉规定的以外一律不许；习俗类：除安拉禁止的以外一律不禁。本题考的是后者——习俗类。）

## Q004

**题目 (Question):**

Persisting in praying in the Cave of Hira is not regarded as an innovation. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

False.

> 说明：在 Hira 山洞坚持礼拜被视为异端（bidah），故原句「不被视为异端」为假。

---

## Q005

**题目 (Question):**

We may not need clear proof to deem a Muslim to have gone beyond the bounds of Islam. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

False.

> 说明：判断一名穆斯林已脱离伊斯兰（kufr/叛教）**必须**有明确证据（clear proof），不可无凭据妄断。故原句「可能不需要明确证据」为假。

---

## Q006

**题目 (Question):**

Circumambulating (tawaf) the graves of righteous men whilst offering supplication to them and asking of them are not innovations that constitute disbelief (kufr). (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

False.

> 说明：绕坟墓环游（tawaf）并向亡人祈祷、向其祈求，这些**是**构成不信（kufr）的异端（bidah），故原句「不是…不信的异端」为假。

---

## Q007

**题目 (Question):**

Ibn Abd al-Barr said: The Quran and Sunnah forbid accusing a Muslim of being an evildoer or disbeliever, in very clear texts in which there is no ambiguity. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

True.

## Q008

**题目 (Question):**

Ash-Shawkani said: You should know that it is not appropriate for a Muslim who believes in Allah and the Last Day to deem another Muslim to have gone beyond the bounds of Islam and become a disbeliever, unless he has proof that is clearer than the light of day. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

True.

## Q009

**题目 (Question):**

The conditions of deeming someone to be a disbeliever include:

**答案 (Answer):**

both of these.

> 💡 助记：判定某人 kufr 的两大条件 = ① 证据（proof）确已到达他 + ② 确凿证明他做了/说了构成不信的事。

---

## Q010

**题目 (Question):**

The basic principle regarding acts of worship is that they are allowed even without a proof. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

False.

> 说明：敬拜类（ibadat）的基本原则是「**无证据则不允许**」，而非「无证据也允许」。原句为假。对比：习俗类（adat）才是「除非禁止否则允许」。

---

## Q011

**题目 (Question):**

Reciting dhikr consisting of a single word, such as repeating "Hu, Hu (He, He)":

**答案 (Answer):**

is one of the innovations which was never narrated from the Prophet ﷺ.

## Q012

**题目 (Question):**

The serious consequences of deeming a specific individual to be a disbeliever include: meat slaughtered by him will not be halal and no funeral prayer will be offered for him if he dies. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

True.

> 助记：错误判定某人为 kufr 的严重后果之一 = 他宰的肉不再清真、死后不得为其举行殡礼（janazah）。

---

## Q013

**题目 (Question):**

What is meant by the Prophet's words "Whoever sets a good precedent in Islam will have the reward for that and the reward of those who do it after him" is:

**答案 (Answer):**

the one who revives a proven Sunnah to which there is a clear reference in Islamic teachings will have the reward of that.

> ⚠️ 易混点：这则圣训指的是「**复兴有明确依据的已证 Sunnah**」，不是「发明新异端并让人效仿」——后者是 bidah，无赏反而有罪。

---

## Q014

**题目 (Question):**

Innovation is not a cause of incurring divine wrath. (True/False)

**答案 (Answer):**

False.

> 说明：异端（bidah）**是**招致神怒（divine wrath）的原因之一，故原句「异端不会招致神怒」为假。

---

## Q015

**题目 (Question):**

Ibn Hajar said that whoever invents something in the religion for which there is no proof from the religious texts to support it:

**答案 (Answer):**

no attention is to be paid to it.

> 助记：Ibn Hajar — 在宗教中无经训依据地新创某事，**不予理会**（no attention is to be paid to it），即使心意真诚也不被接受。

---

## Q016

**题目 (Question):**

The impediments to deeming someone to be a disbeliever (takfeer) include:

**答案 (Answer):**

error.

> 说明：**错误（error）**是阻碍判 kufr 的因素之一——因误解、无知或失误所致，不轻易判其叛教。

---

## Q017

**题目 (Question):**

The basic principle regarding customs and worldly matters is that they are:

**答案 (Answer):**

permissible.

> 助记：习俗与世俗事务（customs/worldly matters）的基本原则 = **允许（permissible）**，除非有证据禁止。对比敬拜类则是「无证据则不允许」。

---

## Q018

**题目 (Question):**

The conditions of deeming someone to be a disbeliever include:

**答案 (Answer):**

it must be proven that this word or action, or this refusal to do something, constitutes disbelief according to the evidence of the Quran and Sunnah.

> 助记：判 kufr 的核心条件 = 依据《古兰》与圣训**确证**该言行/拒绝行为构成不信。

---

## Q019

**题目 (Question):**

Innovation (bidah) in religious matters is _______ in all cases.

**答案 (Answer):**

prohibited.

> 说明：宗教事务中的异端（bidah）在任何情况下都是**被禁止的（prohibited）**，非可憎、非可嘉。

---

## Q020

**题目 (Question):**

The ruling on using air conditioning is:

**答案 (Answer):**

both of these.

> 助记：使用空调 ① 不是异端（bidah）② 是允许的（permissible）——两者皆对。属于世俗事务，非宗教创新。
